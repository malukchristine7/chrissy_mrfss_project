4. Gaps and challenges

Based on the available business information and customer reviews, you can identify these potential gaps:

Gap / Challenge	What it means
Customer ordering	Customers may benefit from a more convenient digital ordering system rather than relying mainly on calls/messages.
Online visibility	The restaurant has an online presence, but its menu and business information could be made easier to access in one place.
Menu availability	Customers have mentioned some dishes being unavailable at times, suggesting a need for better stock/menu availability management. 
Service speed	Some reviews mention that service can sometimes be slow. 
Customer feedback	Reviews provide useful feedback, but a structured system could help management collect and analyze customer complaints and suggestions.
Event management	Fig Tree hosts meetings, parties and other events, so managing bookings, schedules and event enquiries digitally could improve organization. 
Stock management	A digital system could help track food ingredients and identify items that are running low before they run out.
Marketing	A stronger digital marketing and customer-retention system could help attract repeat customers.

5. Possible solutions for each challenge

1. Digital ordering system
    Create a website/mobile-friendly ordering system where customers can view the menu, place orders and receive order confirmations.
2. Digital menu
    Create an updated online menu with food photos, descriptions and prices. A QR-code menu could also be placed on tables.
3. Inventory management system
    Build a system that tracks ingredients and alerts staff when stock is low.
4. Customer feedback system
    Add a simple online feedback form where customers can rate food, service and cleanliness.
5. Online booking system
    Create a booking system for tables, meetings, parties, weddings and other events.
6. Customer database/loyalty system
    Keep records of returning customers and offer loyalty rewards or special promotions.
7. Digital marketing dashboard
    Help management track social-media posts, promotions, customer engagement and campaign performance.
